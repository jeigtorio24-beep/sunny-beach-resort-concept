03 · Plan a trip

Three steps
to the beach.

  1. Pick your dates

    A weekend away, a family reunion or a barkada trip. Know your dates and how many are coming.

  2. Call or message

    Reach the resort directly by phone, email or Facebook. No booking site in between.

  3. Ask what's open

    Ask which rooms or cottages are free, whether a day tour or an overnight stay suits you, and today's rate.

Rates change with the season, so the resort will give you today's price.
